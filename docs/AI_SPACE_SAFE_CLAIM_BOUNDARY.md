# AI Space Safe Claim Boundary

Allowed external wording after the relevant gates pass:

```text
Digua AI-NAS supports local AI Space organization, local image semantic search,
local non-identifying person-attribute search, subtitle text indexing, and
virtual smart categories. Results include evidence references and keep paths
redacted.
```

Do not claim:

- face recognition
- family member identity recognition
- age, gender, race, ethnicity, emotion, health, or disability inference
- cloud vision or cloud ASR
- automatic delete, move, or rename
- complete replacement of a commercial NAS OS
- perfect understanding of all images and videos
